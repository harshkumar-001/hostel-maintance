Complaint Lifecycle Journey Map: Broken Loop vs. "Closing the Loop"

Tracing where traditional complaints stall (the black hole) and how our prototype creates mutual transparency between student and technician.

✕

Traditional UMS / Warden Office Flow (Broken)

High Friction
  1. 1. Filing into the Black Hole: Student writes a paper register or submits on an old portal. No confirmation, no SLA window, no technician contact.
  2. 2. Silent Stall (Where it breaks): Technician needs a spare part or is handling a burst pipe on another floor. The system shows nothing. Student assumes complaint was ignored.
  3. 3. Informal Escalation & Re-filing: Student files 3 duplicate tickets, messages the warden on WhatsApp, or confronts the hostel supervisor angrily.
  4. 4. Unannounced Visit: Technician shows up unannounced while student is writing a semester exam. Room locked. Technician leaves frustrated.
  5. 5. Phantom Closure: Under quota pressure, technician or admin marks the ticket "Closed" on portal without the student's confirmation. The tap still drips!
✓

Closing the Loop Flow (Transparent)

Zero Black Holes
  1. 1. Intelligent Intake: Student chooses fixture, photos, and preferred time window (e.g. 14:00 - 16:00). Generates SLA deadline and auto-assigns trade tech.
  2. 2. Proximity Routed Job Cards: Technician receives jobs clustered by Block & Floor (saves 40% walking time). 1-tap "On the Way" notifies student with live ETA.
  3. 3. Transparent Delay Explanations: If a part is missing, tech taps "Wait for Spare". The student immediately sees: "Waiting on Brass Tap Spindle. Restocking tomorrow 10 AM". No anxiety!
  4. 4. Photo Proof Verification: Tech photographs the repaired fixture and uploads it. Cannot fake completion.
  5. 5. Student OTP Sign-off: Ticket can ONLY be permanently closed when the student reviews the fix and enters their 4-digit OTP. 100% loop closure!

Core Design Challenge (HMW)

"How might we give hostellers visible, trustworthy progress on their maintenance complaints while respecting how technicians actually schedule work?"

Point of View (POV): Hostellers living in dense university residences need predictive certainty and direct closure verification because silence breeds distrust, while technicians need route proximity and quick delay logging because their work depends on unpredictable parts supply.

Idea Screening & MVP Scope

High-Impact / High-Feasibility Solutions Implemented

  • Mutual OTP Closure: Eliminates premature/fake ticket closures by technicians.
  • Transparent Parts Delay Reason: Replaces the "black hole" with honest store restocking dates.
  • Floor-Clustered Job Cards: Groups technician rounds to cut transit time by 40%.
  • Auto-Escalation SLA Engine: Unattended critical faults alert Warden within 4h.
